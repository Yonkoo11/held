## The metaphor, at length, because the mark depends on it

Money leaves the buyer. It does not arrive. It stops at a known point on the path and waits
there while a person decides. That much is ordinary escrow.

The part that is not ordinary is what happens when nobody decides. A card authorisation that
nobody captures expires **backwards**: the hold drops, the payer keeps the money. Held expires
**forwards**: a Hedera scheduled transaction pays the seller at the deadline. Silence is not a
refund here. Silence is delivery.

So the mark is not about containment. A vault, a lock and a shield all say "this is not going
anywhere", which is the opposite of true. The mark is about **momentum that has been paused but
not reversed**, and the drawing has to carry direction, or it is drawing the wrong product.

## What the mark must be consistent with

The live page's signature element is a wire: two labelled nodes, `you` and `the seller`, joined
by a rail, with a pod sitting on the rail carrying the amount, the escrow id and the clock. The
rail is already the product's visual idea at page scale. The mark is that same object at icon
scale, not a different object placed next to it.

`ai/design-progress.md` records the chosen direction as B-structure with A's paper palette and
C's treatment of the countdown as a typographic object, and names the signature element as the
thing that moves without interaction. The mark inherits that direction rather than opening a new
one, per the skill's rule about a direction already on disk.
